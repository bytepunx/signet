## Decisions

- Task layers: 1 T-0013 (stream behaviour, gRPC implementation, fakes); 2 T-0014 watcher and T-0015 lock, both after T-0013 because both are written against the behaviour and the fakes; 3 T-0016 facade and `wait_for_restart/6`, after both because it composes them; 4 T-0017 docs and example, after T-0016 because they describe the finished API. Layer 2's tasks share no path but are run one after the other: the worktree has one `_build/` and concurrent `mix compile` runs in it would corrupt it.
- Module layout follows single responsibility: `Signet.Restart` (facade), `Signet.Restart.Watcher`, `Signet.Restart.Lock`, `Signet.Restart.Streams` (behaviour with a gRPC implementation) under `lib/signet/restart/`, and tests under `test/signet/restart/`, matching the `test/signet/` layout S-0001 chose rather than the flat `test/restart_test.exs` the story's touches name.
- Streaming I/O goes behind the `Signet.Restart.Streams` behaviour, as `Signet.Spiffe.WorkloadAPI` does for the Workload API, so the watcher and lock are tested against `Signet.FakeRestartStreams` without gRPC (code-quality.md: no direct third-party I/O).
- Go's `<-chan struct{}` of capacity 1 becomes one `{:bundle_changed, watcher}` message per burst: the watcher sends it when a change arrives with none outstanding, and sends the next only after the owner acknowledges with `Signet.Restart.next_change/2` (receive then acknowledge) or `Signet.Restart.acknowledge/1` from a `handle_info`. Durations are milliseconds, as Elixir timeouts are; the wire TTL is whole seconds (minimum 1) and the heartbeat interval is TTL/4 of the millisecond value.
- Loss is reported once as `{:lock_lost, lock, reason}` to the process that acquired the lock, never after `release/1`; `release/1` closes the stream (half-close with `GRPC.Stub.end_stream/1`) and the lock process stays alive, linked to its owner, so `token/1` and `expires_at/1` keep answering and a second `release/1` is a no-op.
- Overlap with S-0003 on README, CHANGELOG, and user docs: proposed in MS-0001 that each story edits disjoint sections and the second to merge rebases.
